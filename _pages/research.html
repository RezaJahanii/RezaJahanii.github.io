Decentralized Federated Learning in Mobile Networks

Resolving Mobility-Induced Catastrophic Forgetting in DFL under mobile wireless networks

  • Resolving catastrophic forgetting caused by mobile client departure from the neighborhood.
  • Utilizing Synthetic Sample Distillation and Enriched Knowledge Distillation through Relayed Mobile Caching Mechanism.

Mobility-Assisted Decentralized Federated Learning: Convergence Analysis and A Data Driven Approach

  • Investigating the impact of mobility on Decentralized Federated Learning (DFL) systems.
  • Deriving onvergence analysis for DFL systems under wireless mobile networks and establishing convergence guarantees.
  • Designing a mobility-aware DFL framework to combat network sparsity and data heterogeneity.

Publication: Jahani, Reza, et al. "Mobility-Assisted Decentralized Federated Learning: Convergence Analysis and A Data-Driven Approach." arXiv preprint arXiv:2512.24694 (2025).

Distribution-Aware Mobility Assisted Decentralized Federated Learning

  • Developing heterogeneity-aware trajectories for DFL under mobile wireless networks to resolve the performance degradation under data heterogeneity.

Publication: M. F. Reza, R. Jahani, R. Jin and H. Dai, "Distribution-Aware Mobility-Assisted Decentralized Federated Learning," ICASSP 2026 - 2026 IEEE International Conference on Acoustics, Speech and Signal Processing (ICASSP), Barcelona, Spain, 2026, pp. 3891-3895, doi: 10.1109/ICASSP55912.2026.11464700.

Deep Learning Based 2D Direction of Arrival Estimation using L-shaped Arrays

  • Developed a deep learning-based method for Direction of Arrival (DOA) estimation operating based on observation matrix of transmitted signals by RF sources in a multi-source scenario, outperforming state-of-the-art methods in accuracy and robustness.

Publication: ScienceDirect: Signal Processing (2024)
